Stages fold their outputs into a new manifest (/out/<stage>/<task>) and a program's writes land beside them; the bundle's own files (/program.wasm, /manifest.json, anything under /in/) are always there.         Ledger open ↗

        hashes, not bytes — nothing settled yet

        The ledger is what the control plane actually holds about finished work: for every settled task, the node that produced the accepted result, the 64-hex hash of the output, its size, and where the bytes live. The bytes themselves never pass through the control plane — a node uploads them to the store by hash and reports the hash; the dashboard fetches and re-hashes them before painting. A tick marks a task another node recomputed and agreed on. Newest first.
